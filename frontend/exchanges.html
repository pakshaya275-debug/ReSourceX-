Resource Exchange

Offer a resource you own in return for another resource. Both participants must confirm the handover and receipt before an exchange is marked complete. Your pickup location stays hidden until the offer is accepted.

Location privacy

Exact pickup location is shared with exchange participants only after acceptance and only when you choose “Exact”. “Approximate” and “None” keep the saved location hidden; use participant messaging to coordinate pickup.

Propose an exchange

Choose one of your available resources and an available resource owned by another participant.

Only resources you own can be offered. Resources involved in active exchanges cannot be edited or deleted.
